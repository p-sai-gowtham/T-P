1. Introduction
The Savings and Expenses Tracker is a web application designed to help you keep a daily record of your financial transactions. Whether you want to track your savings, expenses, or both, this tool is perfect for managing your finances and gaining insights into your spending habits. With a user-friendly interface and comprehensive reporting features, it's easy to take control of your finances and make informed decisions.

2. Features
User Authentication: Securely create an account and log in to access your financial data.
Transaction Logging: Add your daily savings and expenses transactions with ease.
Categorization: Organize your transactions by categories for better financial insights.
Reports: Generate reports to view your financial trends and make informed decisions.
Budget Planning: Set savings goals and track your progress.
Responsive Design: Use the application on both desktop and mobile devices.
